The long way home

III. The edge of the water

The path followed the shore until the houses disappeared behind the hill. At that hour the water was almost still, disturbed only by the slow arrival of a boat from the opposite bank.

She stopped beneath the trees and opened the book where she had left it the evening before. A thin piece of paper marked the place. Beyond the page, the light moved steadily across the lake.

There was no need to hurry. The village would still be there when she returned, and the road that climbed through the fields would be as familiar in the evening as it had been in the morning.

For a while she listened to the water. Then she began to read.

By noon, the small sounds of the shore had become part of the silence. She turned another page.
