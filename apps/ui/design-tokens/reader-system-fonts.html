Chapter three

A quiet morning

The garden was still when she arrived. Along the low stone wall, the last of the rain gathered on the leaves, catching the morning light before falling into the grass.

She set her book on the wooden bench and listened. Somewhere beyond the apple trees, a bird was trying the same small phrase, pausing each time as though waiting for an answer.

There was nothing to hurry towards. The path curved between the flower beds and disappeared behind an old gate. For the first time in weeks, she was content to leave it unexplored.
