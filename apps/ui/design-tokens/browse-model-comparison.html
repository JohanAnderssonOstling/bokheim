The shared result contract

Library and catalog produce the same shape while keeping their source rules and card actions.

Query
Source, scope, normalized filters and sorting.
Revision
Source generation, transactional dependency counters and taxonomy revision. Cover and sync bookkeeping preserve results; relevant writes expire them.
Selection
Item identities and membership references.
Ordering
Heading order and item order for requested sections.
Counts
Exact totals, section counts and reusable facets.
Lifetime
Up to eight results, with an estimated 16 MiB budget. Least recently used results are evicted when limits are exceeded; oversized results are rebuilt instead of retained.

Cover rows stay paged. Per-section ordering can be prepared on demand. This contract does not require sorting every section or copying every book into every ancestor.

Membership is many-to-many

A book can appear under several authors or subjects. Count distinct items within each section; section totals are not additive.

Navigation is its own structure

Keep subject route occurrences and compaction separate from filtered counts. A reading-state change need not rebuild the hierarchy.

Identity follows the view

Authors and subjects generally list books or entries. Folder rows identify placements, so moving one copy targets the right directory.

What has already improved

Catalog author index benchmark: 5,520 entries, 4,707 authors. Initial response includes 24 headings and 95 cover rows. Result reuse is now implemented: Author membership is now lazy, facets are cached independently, and background bookkeeping preserves prepared results.
