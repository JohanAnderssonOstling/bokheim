Chapter three

The shape of a
quiet afternoon

There was a certain hour when the light settled on the windowsill and the town seemed to pause. Beyond the garden, the path continued between the trees, turning slowly out of sight.

She had brought a book, although she had not yet opened it. The river was close enough to hear, a steady sound beneath the movement of the leaves.

The book lay open at a drawing of Earth and Mercury. She followed the thin lines around the planets, then looked up at the afternoon sky.

When she finally turned the first page, the afternoon had become a place of its own. The words and the garden belonged to the same quiet silence.
