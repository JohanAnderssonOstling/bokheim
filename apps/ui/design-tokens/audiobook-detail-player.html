    About this book

    When Mary Lennox arrives at her uncle’s house on the Yorkshire moors, she finds a place full of secrets: a hundred rooms, a crying voice in the night, and a garden locked away for ten years.

    With a robin for a guide and an unexpected friendship, Mary begins to discover what can grow when something forgotten is given a little care.

    A story of friendship, belonging, and the quiet work of coming back to life.
