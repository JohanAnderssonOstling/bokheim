Field Notes / Chapter one

The river at dusk

The path followed the river past the last of the houses. The day's warmth had settled into the stones, and the branches above the water were very still.

For a moment the river held the whole evening in its surface. We stopped at the bridge, with nowhere particular left to go.

Across the river, a light appeared in an upstairs window. It was an ordinary thing, but in the gathering blue it seemed enough.
