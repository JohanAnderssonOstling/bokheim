“Come, Lizzy,” said her sister, “you must tell us what you think of him.”

Elizabeth smiled. She had observed enough to form an opinion, but had no wish to share it so readily.

The company had been in excellent spirits, and every new acquaintance supplied a fresh subject for conversation.

“Miss Bennet has a great deal to say,” someone remarked, “when she chooses to say it.”

She returned to her book, content to let the others settle the matter among themselves.
