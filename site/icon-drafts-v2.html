Bokheim — icon drafts

Four directions, one flat colour each. Every mark sits inside a centred circle at 66% of the canvas, so an Android circular or squircle mask will not clip it. Judge these at 32 and 16 px first — that is where marks fail.

1g — Solid book, thin squiggly lines (latest)

The 1d idea rebuilt on the 1f outline: the page block is solid dark with straight edges, and three thin lines per side are cut out of it. The lines are 7 units instead of 15, they waver, and they stop short of the spine so the fold stays solid. The apex is raised and the slope steepened to 47° from 37°. Enlarged 1.25× and refitted from the measured bounding box, so it spans 41–471 across and 85–427 down. The small-size variant is the same block with the lines dropped — a house, which is fine at that size.

1e — Fanned page block, straight edges

Four dark bands and three light gaps in a 1:1 ratio, all thin at the fold and widening toward the tips, so the mark converges to its finest point at the peak instead of massing there. The gaps run the whole way to the fold and meet their mirrors across it. Outer corners are rounded rather than cut flat, and the window is tucked up under the peak. Scaled to 82% so the corners clear a circular mask.

1t — Face-down open book, outlined planes

Each slope is now a page plane with a visible depth, not a bare edge: an outlined quadrilateral with three straight rules of equal length inside it standing for lines of text. 1c — Open book tent, curved page blocks
