# DDC 23 summary captions

`ddc23-summaries.csv` contains the three DDC 23 summary levels published by
OCLC for browsing: 10 main classes, 100 divisions, and 1,000 sections.

The captions were mechanically extracted from OCLC's public “DDC 23
Summaries” PDF, modified 2024-08-01. They are browsing summaries and do not
necessarily match the complete schedule headings. The complete DDC schedules
are not included.
