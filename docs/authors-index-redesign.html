The authors index, with each name carrying two of its covers

Direction A without the A–Z jump rail: the alphabet strip is gone, and the space the avatar disc held goes to two real covers side by side. A row is a pair of covers and a name — no book count, no titles. The letter dividers stay, since they are what makes the list scannable, and so do the sort control and the collapsed catalogue search.

Drawn at 1 : 1. A 200 px cover below is 200 px on your screen; the nav rail is 96 px; the window is 1280 × 800. Scroll the frame sideways if your browser is narrower.

What the row is made of

PartSizeWhy
Row88 px tall, 9 px side paddingUp from 64. Two covers at the 1 : 1.6 ratio need 77 px of height, and a 64 px row would crop them to postage stamps.
Cover pair2 × 48 × 77, 4 px apartThe author's two most recently read books, falling back to the first two by title. The same ratio as every other cover in the app, so the pair reads as books rather than as an icon.
Second cover missingdashed placeholderAn author with one book, or one thumbnail, keeps the row's rhythm instead of letting the name slide left.
Name15 px, medium, one lineSort form — Ekman, Kerstin — so the letter dividers mean something. The only text in the row.
Letter divider12 px mono, rule underSticky at the top of the list while its letter is on screen.

What the taller row costs. 88 px against 64 px is nine rows visible in an 800 px window where the current list shows twelve, before dividers. That is the trade the cover pair asks for, and the reason the A–Z rail existed in the first version — without it, reaching T is a scroll rather than a click.

If that bites, the cheapest recovery is a compact mode: one 48 × 77 cover instead of two, row back to 72 px, nothing else changed.

Still open. No count is rendered anywhere on the page now, so Most books sorts by a number the reader cannot see. Either that chip goes and the index is alphabetical only, or the sort itself has to be the explanation.

Not answered from the code

  • Is sort_name populated in practice? Written from the EPUB's file-as when present. If most books omit it, the dividers need a derived fallback, and that choice is the difference between a day and a week.
  • Which two covers? Drawn here as the two most recently read. Most recently added is a cheaper query; first two by title is cheapest and the least interesting.
  • What do the row thumbnails cost? Covers are already thumbnailed and cached per book, but the index would now load two per author on first paint — a few hundred small reads where today it loads none.
