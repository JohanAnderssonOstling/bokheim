On 14 October, Dr. Andersson opened the old library at half past eight. “Are we ready?” she asked. The temperature was 12.5 degrees, but sunlight warmed the room. A reader paused beside the window, considered the unfamiliar name on the cover, and began again. Some sentences were short. Others unfolded slowly, with a quiet interruption in the middle, before arriving at their final thought. Outside, the rain had stopped. “Take your time,” she said. “There is another chapter tomorrow.” He turned the page and continued reading, listening to the rhythm of the words and the silence between them.
